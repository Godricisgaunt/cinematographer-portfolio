Kenneth Platero-Delucca is a Director of Photography based in Texas whose work centers on character, atmosphere, and emotional perspective. Drawing from naturalistic lighting, intimate compositions, and a strong sense of place, his images explore the relationship between environment and psychology across drama, thriller, horror, commercial, and music video projects.
